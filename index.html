So how do you make a team 10x faster?

Find the work people repeat every week, then make a machine do it. Audits, reports, monitoring, QA and research briefs become tools and agents. People keep the decisions.

Is Anton an SEO person or an automation person?

Both, on purpose. Years of hands-on SEO at scale tell me which problems are worth automating. The engineering side lets me ship the fix myself instead of filing a ticket.

Does this only work for SEO?

No. The same approach works for any product or operations process with data, repetition and a clear definition of “done”. SEO is just where I proved it first.
