SEO OS — the in-house SEO platform

Problem
SEO data lived in 25+ tools. Audits, reports and checks were done by hand, and some problems were simply too big to see by eye.
What I did
Designed and built a Django platform alone: 67 active modules, 50 scheduled jobs, 25+ integrations (Search Console, BigQuery, GA4, CrUX, Bing, Ahrefs, Serpstat, Jira, Slack, LLMs), ~1,800 automated tests. 800+ commits in four months.
Result
Used daily by the SEO team and the Chief Growth Officer. A technical audit of 11 page types on every locale host runs every day (~1,100 headless renders) and caught issues a person would not. Example: a US edge migration that served empty pages to ~12% of Googlebot fetches and broke every sitemap on the main market. It was found by the platform, not by a person, and fixed: empty pages went from 12.4% to 0.5%. A 156 GB local mirror of Search Console answers any traffic question without API limits.
